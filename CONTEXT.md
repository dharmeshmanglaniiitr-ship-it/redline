# Redline

Redline reads a document someone else drafted and tells the person about to sign it
what they're actually agreeing to.

## Language

**Signer**:
The person using Redline — about to sign a document they did not draft. Redline's v1
signer is a freelancer or independent contractor.
_Avoid_: User, reader (too generic — "signer" carries that this person has something
at stake in the document, not just reading it).

**Sender**:
The other party to the document — whoever drafted it and gave it to the signer to sign.
For Redline's v1 freelancer signer, the sender is normally their client.
_Avoid_: Counterparty (too legal-sounding for a plain-English product), other party.

**Leverage**:
Whether the signer has a realistic path to negotiate the document's terms before
signing, as opposed to a take-it-or-leave-it document like a consumer ToS. Determines
whether a drafted counter-offer is the product's core value (high leverage) or mostly
advisory (low leverage). Redline v1 is built for signers with real leverage.
_Avoid_: Bargaining power (interchangeable, but "leverage" is the term this repo has
settled on — keep it consistent).
